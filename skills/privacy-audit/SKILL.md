# Privacy Audit

Use before enabling a new service, MCP, skill, sync target or public endpoint.

1. Identify what data leaves the host.
2. List credentials/scopes required.
3. Check listening interfaces and authentication.
4. Identify write/destructive capabilities.
5. Check logs/telemetry/retention.
6. Prefer localhost or tailnet exposure.
7. Give a risk rating: low / medium / high / unacceptable.
8. Recommend the minimum-authority configuration.
